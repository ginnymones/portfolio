Beginner's setup guides

Two job search automations

Claude can do the daily legwork of a job search for you: finding roles that fit, keeping your tracker tidy and emailing you a short summary. Pick the one that matches how you look for work. Either way, you decide what to apply to and submit every application yourself.

Job board update

For a traditional job search. Every day, Claude finds new roles that match your Settings tab, rates how well each one fits, adds them to your Job Board sheet, tidies your tracker and emails you a summary.

Upwork job digest

For freelancing. Every weekday morning, Claude picks up to five Upwork jobs that fit your profile, explains why, saves them to a Google Sheet and emails you. It writes a proposal only when you ask.

Guide 1 of 2

Your daily job board update

Every day, Claude searches for new roles that match the criteria in your Google Sheet, checks each one against your rules, rates how well it fits and adds it to your Job Board tab. It also tidies your tracker for you and emails a short summary.

What it does each day

Seven jobs, done before you open your laptop

  1. Reads the Settings tab of your sheet to learn what you're looking for: roles, location, shifts, salary range and companies to avoid.
  2. Tidies your tracker first. Rows you marked Applied move to your Job Tracker, and rows you marked Closed or Not Applied move to the Archive.
  3. Searches for roles posted in the last seven days, using a design-jobs connector alongside regular web search.
  4. Reads your JobStreet email alerts in Gmail, if you use JobStreet and have switched this on.
  5. Checks each role against your rules: no duplicates, salary in range, no excluded companies, and a work setup and shift that suit you.
  6. Rates each new role High, Medium or Low, gives the reason for any Medium or Low, and adds a short company review summary with links.
  7. Adds the new roles to your Job Board and emails you the summary.

It never applies, messages recruiters or fills in forms for you. It only reads, searches and keeps your sheet tidy.

Before you start

Four things you need

The most important is knowing what you want. Claude only finds what your Settings tab describes, so spend a few minutes getting it right.

A Claude account

On a plan that includes connectors and scheduled tasks. If you can't find these options later, check your plan in Claude's settings.

A Google account

Your tracker lives in Google Sheets and your daily summary arrives in Gmail.

Your search criteria

The roles you want, where you can work from, your preferred shift, a salary floor and any companies to skip.

Optional: JobStreet alerts

If you use JobStreet, saved-search alert emails let Claude see its listings without visiting the site.

Setup

Five steps, about 30 minutes

1

Connect your apps

Claude needs permission to read and write your Google Sheet and to read and send Gmail. You give it once, and it takes about five minutes.

  1. Open Claude and start a new chat.
  2. Type: "Help me connect Google Sheets, Gmail and a design jobs search connector."
  3. Claude shows a card with a Connect button for each app. Click each one.
  4. A sign-in window opens. Log in with your own Google account and click Allow.
  5. When everything shows as connected, you're done.

If you'd rather do it yourself, look for Connectors in Claude's settings. Menu names can change, so if you can't find it, ask Claude where it is.

Connecting doesn't let Claude apply to anything. The instructions in step four tell it to only search, read and update your sheet.

5

Optional: turn on JobStreet alerts

JobStreet blocks automated visits to its website, but it can email you new listings. Claude reads those emails instead. Skip this step if you don't use JobStreet, and set the Settings switch to Off.

  1. Sign in to JobStreet and search for a role you want, with your location.
  2. Click Save search or Create job alert, and choose daily emails.
  3. Repeat for each of your main roles. Each saved search adds more coverage.
  4. Make sure the alerts go to the Gmail address you connected, and that Settings says On.

The alerts come from broad searches, so many listings will be unrelated. Claude keeps only the ones that match your target roles. They don't show the full posting, so Claude marks the setup or shift as unconfirmed and rates those roles Medium at most. Open the listing yourself to check before you apply.

Every day

Read your summary and your Job Board

Each day you get one email titled "Job board update: [date]", and the new roles appear at the bottom of your Job Board. Spend 10 to 15 minutes on it.

  1. Read the email first. It lists the new roles, how many rows moved, and anything that failed.
  2. Open the Job Board and read the Match Confidence and the reason behind every Medium or Low.
  3. Open the listing using the link and read the full post yourself. Check the company reviews too.
  4. Decide. If you apply, type Applied in the Status cell. If a role isn't for you, type Not Applied. Claude handles the rest on the next run.

Good habits

Tips for applying and staying safe

The best habit is to apply early to fewer, better-matched roles, and to check every listing yourself before you share any details.

Getting the most from it

  • Apply within a day or two of a role appearing. Listings fill up quickly.
  • Start with the High matches, then look at Mediums whose reason you can live with.
  • Keep your Settings tab current. When your rules change, your results change.
  • Check unfamiliar companies yourself, especially when review data is thin.
  • Use the Notes column in your Job Tracker to record contacts, interview dates and follow-ups.

Staying safe

  • Never pay anyone to apply, interview or start a job, whatever the reason given.
  • Be wary of listings that move you to a messaging app before any interview.
  • Don't share ID documents or bank details until you have a written offer from a company you've verified.
  • Apply through the company's own careers page when you can.
  • Treat a Low rating as a prompt to look twice, not as a verdict.
Later on

Changing the rules

Most changes need no instructions at all: just edit the value in your Settings tab, and the next run uses it. For changes to the schedule or the way it works, open a chat in Claude, say what you want in plain words, and Claude updates your scheduled task.

"Change my job board update to run at 8 am." "Only run it on weekdays." "Switch the notification to push only." "Look back 14 days on the next run." "Add Behance to the places you search." "Pause my job board update for a week."
If something's off

Troubleshooting

Most problems come from a disconnected app, and the fix is to reconnect it in Claude.

No email today

Check your spam folder and the notification preference in Settings. Then ask Claude, "Did my job board update run today?"

The email says a connector failed

Reconnect that app (step one). Sign-ins can expire.

Nothing appeared in my sheet

Check the sheet link in your instructions and that the tab names match exactly.

"No new listings today"

Normal on quiet days. Claude won't pad the list. Widening your target roles or location scope helps.

The roles don't suit me

Tighten your Settings tab: more specific roles, a clearer location rule, more excluded companies.

No JobStreet results

Check that alerts are arriving in Gmail and that the Settings switch says On.

The task paused for approval

Switch the task to automatic approval in its settings, if your account allows it.

Your weekday Upwork job digest

Every weekday morning, Claude searches Upwork for you and picks up to five new jobs that fit your experience and preferences. It explains why each one suits you, saves everything in a Google Sheet and emails you a summary. When you want a proposal for a job, you ask Claude for one.

What it does each weekday

Six jobs, done before you open your laptop

  1. Reads your Upwork profile and portfolio, so it knows what you're good at.
  2. Searches for jobs posted in the last two days that match your skills.
  3. Skips jobs that aren't worth your time, such as low pay, clients with bad reviews, or posts that already have lots of applicants.
  4. Picks up to five jobs that fit you best and explains why each one suits your experience and what you're looking for, including its main weak point.
  5. Re-checks jobs from earlier days and moves ones that are no longer worth it into a separate Closed list.
  6. Emails you a copy of all of it.

It never applies, spends Connects or messages clients for you. It only writes a proposal when you ask for one.

Moves it to the Closed tab.

Keeps checking it each day.

Claude also moves jobs to the Closed tab by itself when they reach 20+ applicants, hire someone, or get closed by the client. Anything archived stays there, with the reason and date.

Good habits

Tips for applying and staying safe

The best habit for a new freelancer is to apply early to fewer, better-matched jobs, and keep every conversation and payment inside Upwork.

Getting hired

  • Apply within a day of a job being posted, while there are under 10 applicants.
  • Spend Connects on the jobs you're most excited about. Don't spread them across every job on the list.
  • Follow the client's instructions exactly. Many posts ask for a word at the start of your proposal to check you read it.
  • Show two or three samples that match the job, not your whole portfolio.
  • Never claim skills or results you don't have. The drafts mark these gaps for you.

Staying safe

  • Keep messages and payment on Upwork. Be wary if a client asks you to move to WhatsApp, Telegram or email before hiring you.
  • Never pay a client for anything, such as "training", "software" or a "starter kit".
  • Don't share ID documents or bank details in messages.
  • On hourly jobs, log your time with Upwork's time tracker. Check Upwork's help pages for how payment protection works for each contract type.
  • Be careful with large unpaid tests. A small paid trial is normal; hours of free work is not.
  • Take the flags seriously. A low client rating or "never hired" is a reason to look twice.
Later on

Changing the rules

You never need to edit the instructions by hand. Open a chat in Claude, say what you want changed in plain words, and Claude updates your scheduled task. The new rules apply from the next run.

"Change my Upwork digest to run at 9 am instead." "Add logo design and Canva to my digest keywords." "Only show jobs paying at least $20 an hour." "Skip jobs that cost more than 15 Connects." "Keep the Why it fits notes to two sentences." "Pause my Upwork digest for a week."
If something's off

Troubleshooting

Most problems come from a disconnected app, and the fix is to reconnect it in Claude.

No email today

Check your spam folder. Then ask Claude, "Did my Upwork digest run today?"

The email says Upwork isn't connected

Reconnect Upwork (step one). Sign-ins can expire.

Nothing appeared in my sheet

Check the sheet link in your instructions, and that Google Sheets is still connected.

Fewer than five jobs listed

Normal on quiet days. Claude won't pad the list with poor matches. Adding keywords helps.

The jobs don't suit me

Update your Upwork profile and portfolio, or ask Claude to change your keywords.

A proposal says something untrue

Delete it before sending, and tell Claude so it can tighten the rules.

The task paused for approval

Switch the task to automatic approval in its settings, if your account allows it.
